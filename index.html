Movie Explorer

Discover and organize your movie collection effortlessly. Movie Explorer scans your internal, external, and network drives for files like MKV, M4V, AVI, and MP4. It builds a searchable offline catalog enriched with metadata from The Movie Database (TMDb), complete with posters, cast photos, and technical details.

Automatic Mapping

Automatically matches your files to movie and TV show data, pulling in original titles, plots, release dates, ratings, cast, crew, and studio info. Reads technical specs via MediaInfo—encoding, bitrate, resolution, audio details—for precise library management.

Rich Visual Gallery

Browse stunning posters, stills, cast and crew photos, and studio logos. View your collection as resizable thumbnails, grouped by actors, directors, studios, or file locations. Open dedicated galleries for deeper exploration.

Advanced Filtering and Search

Customizable sidebar filters by location, genre, country, rating, plus quick filters for recently added, stereoscopic, HD video/audio, and duplicates. Search titles, cast, crew, studios, or filenames. Track watched status with Trakt.tv sync (TV Shows included).

Seamless Playback and Export

Double-click to play with your default app, or use 'Play with' for custom choices. Quick IMDb and homepage links. Export collection data to CSV. All stored locally in a fast, searchable database.

How It Works

Step 1: Scan Your Drives

Add your internal, external, or network drives. Movie Explorer automatically detects and catalogs movie files.

Step 2: Enrich with Metadata

Matches files to TMDb data, fetching posters, details, cast, crew, and technical info. Manual mapping available for precision.

Step 3: Browse and Organize

Filter, search, group, and mark watched. Resize thumbnails, explore galleries, and play files directly.

Step 4: Export and Sync

Export to CSV, sync watch status with Trakt.tv, and link to IMDb or homepages for more.
